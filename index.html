⚡ Taper above 80%: both AC and DC charging slow down significantly above 80% SOC to protect the LFP cells. DC drops from ~82.6 kW to ~25 kW; AC drops from ~6.3 kW to ~3.5 kW. The last 20% takes disproportionately longer — plan accordingly.
For AC charging, the BYD Seal's onboard charger caps acceptance at 7 kW regardless of charger speed, with ~10% conversion loss — so Home and Evro AC give the same charge rate. DC bypasses the onboard charger and accepts up to 150 kW, with ~5% line loss. All times are estimates; real sessions vary with cable rating, charger load, and temperature.
